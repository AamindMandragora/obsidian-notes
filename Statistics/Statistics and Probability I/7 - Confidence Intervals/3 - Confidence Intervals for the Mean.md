A **confidence interval** is a range of numbers believed, with some probability, the **confidence coefficient** $1-\alpha$, to include an unknown population parameter. The interval will take the following form: $$\left(\overline X-z_{\alpha/2}\cdot\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}, \overline X+z_{\alpha/2}\cdot\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\right)$$When $\sigma$ is unknown, we can use the t distribution to get an interval: $$\overline x\pm t_{\alpha/2}\cdot\frac{s}{\sqrt n}$$
