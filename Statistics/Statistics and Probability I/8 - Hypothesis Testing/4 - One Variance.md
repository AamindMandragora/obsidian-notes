Our confidence interval for $\sigma^2$ will be: $$\left(\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_{\alpha/2}},\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_{1-\alpha/2}}\right)$$Left-tailed hypothesis tests have $H_0:\sigma^2\geq \sigma_0^2$, right-tailed tests have $H_0:\sigma^2\leq\sigma_0^2$, and two-tailed tests have $H_0:\sigma^2=\sigma_0^2$.

Our test statistic is $\chi^2=\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma^2}\sim\chi^2_{(n-1)}$.
