If we consider two populations with success proportions $p_1,p_2$, then given the numbers of successes $y_1,y_2$ in the two samples, we have $\hat{p_k}=y_k/n_k$. For large $n_1$ and $n_2$, $(\hat p_1-\hat p_2)$ is approximately distributed normally with mean $p_1-p_2$ and variance $\frac{p_1(1-p_1)}{n_1}+\frac{p_2(1-p_2)}{n_2}$. This means a confidence interval for $p_1-p_2$ is $$(\hat p_1+\hat p_2)\pm z_{\alpha/2}\cdot\sqrt{\frac{p_1(1-p_1)}{n_1}+\frac{p_2(1-p_2)}{n_2}}$$and our test statistic is $$Z=\frac{\hat p_1-\hat p_2}{\sqrt{\frac{p_1(1-p_1)}{n_1}+\frac{p_2(1-p_2)}{n_2}}}$$
