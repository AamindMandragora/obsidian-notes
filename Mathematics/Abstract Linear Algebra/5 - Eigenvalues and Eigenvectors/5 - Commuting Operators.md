Two operators or square matrices of the same size **commute** if $ST=TS$. For example, $\lambda I$ computes with every operator for scalar lambda, and powers of operators commute. Operators commute iff their matrices (with respect to the same basis) commute. For $S,T\in\mathcal L(V)$, if $ST=TS$, $E[\lambda, S]$ is $T$-invariant. $S$ and $T$ have diagonal matrices with respect to the same basis iff they commute. They have a common eigenvector and have upper-triangular matrices in some basis as well. $S+T$ has eigenvalues $\lambda_S+\lambda_T$, and $ST$ has eigenvalues $\lambda_S\lambda_T$.
